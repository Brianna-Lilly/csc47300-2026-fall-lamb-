# Campus Facilities Manager

# 1. Project Description
Campus Facilities Manager is a web application created to keep CCNY students, faculty, and staff members in the loop by providing them with information regarding escalator and elevator service 
in one place. With Campus Facilities Manager, CCNY community members can view the current status of campus elevators and escalators, see how crowded an elevator is, and provide service updates.
This is important because it is not only convenient for staff and students to know if the elevator and escalators are working, but this information is crucial for members of the community who rely on the escalators 
and elevators for accessibility reasons. Our goal is to use Campus Facilities Manager to make CCNY more convenient and accessible.

# 2. Problem
At CCNY, elevator and escalator service are extremely inconsistent. Students and other campus community members never know which elevators and escalators are in service when they arrive to campus
because service varies from day to day. This can be particularly problematic for individuals who rely on elevators for accessibility. Campus Facilities Manager addresses this problem by by providing 
information about equipment status and occupancy.

# 3. Target Users
- Students
- Faculty & Staff Members
- Visitors
- Facilities & Maintenance Staff
- Individuals who rely on the equipment for accessibility reasons
  
# 4. Key Features
## Equipment Status Tracking (Brianna)
Users will be able to view the current status of elevators and escalators, which will be labeled as either in service, under maintenance, or out of service. If the status is incorrect or has abruptly changed, 
students can update the status. 
## Campus Map & Navigation (Abigail)
There will be a visual map of campus consisting of the different buildings on campus. Users will select the building that they want and will see a visual representation of where the elevators and escalators 
in that building are located. 
Since our group name has the initials LAMB, we have decided to go for a lamb/farm theme. This theme will be incorporated with this feature, since each building will be represented by a barn.
## Occupancy & Capacity Tracking (Marina)
Users can update the platform with how crowded an elevator is, which will help to provide an estimate of the amount of people currently on an elevator will be displayed. The lamb/farm theme will be incorporated into this feature since lambs will represent how crowded the elevator is.
- 🐑 Low Traffic
- 🐑🐑🐑 Moderate Traffic
- 🐑🐑🐑🐑🐑 Heavy Traffic
## Campus Equipment Directory (Lin)
Users will have access to a directory that consists of all campus elevators and escalators, where they will be able to view information about each piece of equipment such as the building it's located in.

# 5. UI/Design Concept
Campus Facilities Manager will have a user-friendly interface that will make campus mobility information easy to understand. 
Because our group name is lamb, we are incorporated sheep and farm-themed into our interface. As mentioned before, sheep may be used to represent elevator occupants, while other farm-inspired elements may be incorporated into the campus map and interface.
